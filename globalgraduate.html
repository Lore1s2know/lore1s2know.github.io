Who am I?

I am a cosmopolitan at heart—a believer in the idea that we are citizens of a shared global city. My perspective is shaped by my unique heritage: I was born in Mexico City, carrying the legacy of my father's family that migrated from northern Mexico following the Mexican Revolution in the early 20th century, alongside the resilience of my mother's Spanish-Catalan family who found a new home in Mexico following the WWII and Spanish Civil War conflicts of the 1940s. I am deeply proud of these intersecting Mexican and Spanish roots, which have instilled in me a profound appreciation for the importance of one's cultural upbringing and the geopolitical contexts that shape our world.

I am driven by a passion for fostering connection. Whether through building digital spaces that honor diverse perspectives or supporting community-based social programs, I believe in the power of creating environments where different ways of thinking, living, and being can exchange ideas and grow into a more vibrant, inclusive whole.

Professional Expertise

I am a diligent and technically-driven professional with a dual background in Philosophy and Web Development. My career is defined by transforming standard administrative workflows into automated, high-performance, relational data management systems. Whether optimizing operations for community health center programs such as the CVITP Income Tax Clinic at Pinecrest-Queensway, or helping to integrate and rollout ERP software like Emburse Professional or Sage Intacct, I focus on building systems that provide clarity, efficiency, and real-world impact.

Currently, I am pursuing my PMP certification and the Microsoft Business Analyst Professional Certificate to further bridge the gap between technical data architecture and industry-standard project management practices. My goal is to serve as a strategic link in non-profit and community-focused environments, ensuring that the technology we use actively supports our mission to serve the public good.

My Commitment

Throughout my time at the community level—including my work at the Pinecrest-Queensway Community Health Centre and my engagement with the Algonquin College community—I have seen firsthand that a thriving learning and service environment is built on diversity, cooperation, and respect. I bring that same commitment to every project I undertake, aiming to advocate for stakeholders and improve the systems that connect us all.

I am always open to discussing how my experience in data automation, web architecture, and community leadership can support the goals of your organization.
